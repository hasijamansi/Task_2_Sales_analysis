# Task 2 – Sales Analysis

## Objective
Analyze the Superstore sales dataset using Tableau to identify sales trends, profitability, regional performance, customer segments, and top-performing products.

## Tools Used
- Tableau
- Superstore Dataset

## Analysis Completed
- Created KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Quantity.
- Analyzed sales and profit by category.
- Analyzed sales by region and customer segment.
- Created a sales trend over time.
- Identified the Top 10 products by sales.
- Created dashboards for sales performance analysis.
- Created a Key Business Insights dashboard.
- Created a Tableau Story to summarize the analysis.

## Key Takeaways
- Technology generated the highest sales and profit among the categories.
- The West region recorded the highest sales.
- Consumer was the highest-selling customer segment.
- Sales showed strong growth from 2012 to 2014.
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 was the top-selling product among the Top 10 products.
